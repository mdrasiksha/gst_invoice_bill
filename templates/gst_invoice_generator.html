What is a GST invoice?

A GST invoice is a tax invoice used for taxable supplies of goods or services. A complete invoice normally contains the supplier and customer information, invoice number and date, product or service description, quantity, taxable value, GST rate and the tax breakup.

GST Smart is designed for freelancers, traders, shops and small businesses that want to create clear GST invoices without setting up a complex accounting system.

Common GST invoice details

  • Business name and GSTIN, when applicable
  • Customer name and address
  • Invoice number and invoice date
  • Item or service description
  • HSN/SAC, quantity and unit price
  • GST rate and tax amounts
  • Place of supply and total amount

GST Calculation

CGST + SGST or IGST?

Intra-state supply

When the supplier and place of supply are in the same state, GST is generally split into CGST and SGST.

Inter-state supply

When the supplier and place of supply are in different states, IGST generally applies.
